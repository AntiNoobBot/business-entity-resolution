# Attribution and publication scope

This is an independent participant project related to the Amazon ML Challenge 2026. It is not an official Amazon repository, implementation, or endorsement.

The MIT license covers original code and documentation in this repository. Third-party libraries and pretrained models retain their own licenses. The challenge dataset, official statement, organizer validator/template, pretrained or fine-tuned weight files, and original submission outputs are not included or relicensed here.

The experiment used the `intfloat/multilingual-e5-small` model identifier with Sentence Transformers, PyTorch, and HNSWlib. Other important packages include Polars, NumPy, PyArrow, LightGBM, RapidFuzz, scikit-learn, SciPy, text-unidecode, and sparse-dot-topn. Consult their upstream distribution metadata before redistributing a reconstructed environment or model.

Historical reports are aggregate excerpts. Business names, addresses, record-level examples, and the autogenerated model-card examples were excluded from this public archive. The small `state_map.json` retains learned aggregate address-tail aliases used by the original code; its split limitations are documented.
